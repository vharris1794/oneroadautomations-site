02 No surprises

What you get, and what you don't.

Included in every build

  • Mobile-first design that looks great on phones
  • Fast load times, with no bloated page builder
  • Tap-to-call and a working contact form
  • Basic search setup so Google understands your business
  • Full ownership: the site runs on hosting and a domain in your name

Not included

  • Ongoing maintenance or changes after handoff (available as separately quoted work)
  • Stock photos or paid third-party tools unless added

04 FAQ

Common questions

Who owns the website?

You do. When the project is paid in full, the site goes onto hosting and a domain in your own name, and you get all the logins. Nothing is locked to me.

Will I have any monthly bills?

Not from me. Hosting runs on your own account, usually on a free plan. Your only regular cost is the domain name, about $12–$20 a year, paid to the domain registrar.

How long does it take?

Typically 5–7 business days after I receive your logo, photos, and business details.

What do you need from me?

Your logo (if you have one), a few photos of your work, your services, hours, service area, and how you want customers to reach you. No logo? Add logo design for $150.

Can I make changes later?

This is a one-time project, so ongoing changes aren't included after handoff. Any web developer can update the site, or I can quote changes as separate work.

What if I change my mind?

Deposits reserve my time and aren't refundable once work begins. See the full refund & cancellation policy.
