Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information One Road Automations, operated by Vincent Harris in New York, collects and how it's used.

Information I collect

  • What you send me: your name, business name, email, phone number, and project details when you email, call, or text.
  • Payment details: when you pay, Stripe collects your payment information and shares your name, email, phone, business name, and payment status with me. I never receive or store your full card number.
  • This website: this site doesn't use cookies, analytics, or tracking. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under Google's own privacy policy.

How I use it

  • To reply to you and deliver your project.
  • To send invoices, receipts, and project updates.
  • To keep business and tax records.

Sharing

I don't sell your information. I share it only with services needed to run the business, such as Stripe for payments and my email provider, or when the law requires it.

Retention

I keep project and payment records as long as needed for the project and for tax and legal record-keeping.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information I hold about you, subject to record-keeping requirements. Email info@oneroadautomations.com.
